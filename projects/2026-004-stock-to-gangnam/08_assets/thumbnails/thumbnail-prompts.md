# Thumbnail Prompts

## Option 1

```text
Use case: ads-marketing
Asset type: YouTube thumbnail, 16:9 landscape, high-click Korean finance/real-estate content
Primary request: Create a dramatic, high-contrast thumbnail for a Korean YouTube video about stock profits moving into Gangnam real estate.
Canvas: 16:9 YouTube thumbnail composition, clean and readable on mobile.
Scene/backdrop: Split-screen financial drama. Left side shows a collapsing stock chart and red sell-off candles; right side shows bright luxury Gangnam apartment towers and a glowing Seoul/Gangnam map outline. A thick gold arrow moves from stock market money to Gangnam real estate.
Main text in image, large and exact Korean text: "주식 팔고\n강남 간다?"
Secondary text badge: "3.7조 이동"
Design style: bold Korean YouTube finance thumbnail, premium but provocative, bright background, navy/gold/mint palette, intense contrast, cinematic lighting, glossy 3D infographic look.
Composition: Korean text very large on the left foreground, readable, no tiny text. Big gold number badge near center. Gangnam apartment towers and map on right. Add urgency with red stock-market crash elements on left, gold money flow arrow to right.
Avoid: faces, people, logos, brand marks, clutter, tiny unreadable text, misspelled Korean, dark muddy background, photorealistic humans, watermark.
```

```text
Avoid: faces, people, logos, brand marks, excessive tiny labels, misspelled Korean, dark muddy image, watermark, overly complex background.
```
