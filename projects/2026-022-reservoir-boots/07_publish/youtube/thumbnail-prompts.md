No legs, no extra arms, no gore. Close tight decisive boot-and-arm clue, chilly electric blue water contrast warm distant cabin light. LEFT one-third dark readable negative area with HUGE crisp Korean headline exactly '발이 아니었다' in two lines, warm ivory and one red accent. Four to eight Korean syllables, no other text/logo. High contrast mobile readable, realistic object anatomy, no ending/underbridge reveal.

RIGHT side two EMPTY dark-green rubber boots, one with small square blue tape, walk upright across water toward viewer: open tops visibly EMPTY, no person, leg, feet, arms or ghost shown.
