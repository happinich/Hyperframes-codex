# Shorts V2 ImageGen Prompts

These project-bound images were created with the built-in ImageGen workflow.

## `shorts-v2-double-friend.png`

Vertical photorealistic Korean camping horror frame viewed from inside a warm
orange tent. A male friend sleeps face-hidden in the foreground while an
impossible humanlike imitation occupies the rain-soaked exterior. The zipper
divides the warm interior and cold cyan forest. No text, logo, gore, or visible
face.

## `shorts-v2-six-fingers.png`

Vertical photorealistic creature-horror close-up of an overlong bark-textured
hand emerging through the wet floor seam of an orange tent. Warm lantern rim
light, cold darkness, face-free framing, no text, logo, blood, or gore. The
composition uses an explicit six-count overlay in Hyperframes.

## `shorts-v2-followed-road.png`

Vertical photorealistic night road after the campsite escape. A compact car
descends through wet forest while a too-tall, long-armed silhouette remains
visible behind it. Cold cyan rain, restrained red taillights, unresolved ending,
no faces, text, logo, watermark, or gore.
