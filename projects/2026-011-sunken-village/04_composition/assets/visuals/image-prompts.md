# 장면 이미지 생성 프롬프트

`01_script/visual-reference.md`의 기준을 따른다. 모든 프롬프트에 공통 제약이 이미 포함되어 있다.

- 규격: 1920x1080 이상, 16:9, PNG
- 저장 위치: 이 폴더(`04_composition/assets/visuals/`)에 아래 파일명 그대로
- 공통 금지: 사람 얼굴, 유혈, 문자·로고·워터마크, 무너진 폐허, 밝은 전역 조명

화면 문자(수치, 표, 라벨)는 이미지에 넣지 않는다. 컴포지션에서 HTML로 얹는다.

## 1. `village-revealed-wide.png` — S01, S09

```text
Cinematic wide shot of a Korean mountain village revealed after a reservoir drained
after 30 years. Traditional tile roofs, low earthen walls, and utility poles rise out
of thick wet brown mud. The structures are intact and orderly, not collapsed or
ruined. Overcast late afternoon light, cold desaturated blue-grey sky, wet reflective
mud surface. Deep navy and mud-brown palette. Photorealistic, no people, no text.
```

## 2. `bell-tower-base.png` — S01, S19

```text
Close view of a bare concrete foundation pad where a church bell tower once stood,
now sitting in drained reservoir mud. Rusted anchor bolts protrude from the concrete.
Nothing stands on it. Shallow water pooled around the edges reflects a grey sky.
Cold blue-grey light, wet surfaces, ominous emptiness. Photorealistic, no people,
no text, no bell visible.
```

## 3. `empty-frames-album.png` — S04

```text
Still life of an old photo album lying open with all photographs removed, leaving
pale rectangles on yellowed pages. Two empty wooden picture frames lean nearby.
Dim warm indoor lamp from the left, deep shadows, dust in the air. Muted browns and
near-black background. Photorealistic, no people, no faces, no text.
```

## 4. `living-room-august-rain.png` — S05

```text
A dim Korean apartment living room at night seen from a doorway. One lamp is on.
Heavy rain streaks the window. A wall calendar hangs beside the window. An empty
armchair faces the window. Nobody present. Cold blue window light against warm lamp
light, strong contrast, quiet dread. Photorealistic, no people, no text.
```

## 5. `empty-funeral-hall.png` — S06

```text
An empty Korean funeral hall viewed down a row of vacant folding chairs. A guest book
lies open on a small table in the foreground, pages blank. Fluorescent overhead light,

Photorealistic, no people, no portrait visible, no text.
```

## 6. `red-notebook.png` — S07, S30, S35

```text
A small worn notebook with a faded red cloth cover, bound with thread, lying open on a
dark surface. The visible pages carry rows of handwritten Korean pen strokes rendered
as indistinct ink marks, not legible words. Edges water-stained and slightly warped.
Single warm focused light from upper left, deep shadow around. The red cover is the
only saturated color in frame. Photorealistic macro, no text that can be read.
```

## 8. `control-post-loudspeaker.png` — S10, S11

```text
A weathered reservoir management post beside a dam embankment. A grey loudspeaker horn

```

## 9. `boots-in-mud.png` — S12

```text
Low point-of-view shot of black rubber boots standing in thick wet reservoir mud, seen
from the wearer's own downward gaze. A flashlight beam cuts across the mud to the
right. Footprint depressions filling slowly with water. Cold blue ambient light against
warm flashlight beam. Photorealistic, only boots and lower legs visible, no face,
no text.
```

## 10. `courtyard-jars.png` — S13

```text
Overhead view of a Korean village courtyard after decades underwater. Five large

Orderly and undisturbed, which is what makes it wrong. Photorealistic, no people,
```

## 11. `house-interior-intact.png` — S13, S14

```text
Interior of an abandoned Korean village house recovered from under a reservoir. A
```

## 16. `observation-tower-gauge.png` — S26

```text
A concrete reservoir water-level observation tower rising from shallow water, with a
vertical painted measurement scale on its side. The waterline sits low on the scale.
Overcast sky, cold blue water, wet concrete streaked with old high-water stains.
Shot from the shore at a slight upward angle. Photorealistic, no numbers legible,
no people, no text.
```

## 17. `footprints-on-water.png` — S28

```text
A shallow sheet of water covering a village alley, lit by a flashlight from behind the
viewer. Across the water surface runs a single line of shallow depressions shaped like
footprints, pressed into the water itself rather than the mud below. Each depression
holds a faint ring of disturbance. Night, cold blue, one warm beam. Uncanny and
restrained. Photorealistic, no people, no text.
```

## 18. `kitchen-forty-two-bowls.png` — S29

```text
Interior of a very small Korean village kitchen after decades underwater. Dozens of
identical lidded rice bowls are arranged across a low table, the floor, and the stove
ledge, far more than the room could plausibly serve. All lids are closed. Fine silt
coats everything. One flashlight beam from the doorway. Rising water covers the floor
to ankle depth. Photorealistic, no people, no text.
```

## 19. `chest-deep-water-escape.png` — S33

```text
Point-of-view shot from chest-deep dark water in a flooded village alley at night. A
handheld flashlight beam shakes across the water surface and a nearby earthen wall.
Water is turbid and rising. Motion blur on the beam. Only a hand holding the flashlight
is visible at the frame edge. Cold black-blue, single warm beam. Photorealistic, no
face, no text.
```

## 20. `lanterns-under-water.png` — S34

```text
A dark reservoir surface at night seen from the embankment. Beneath the water, many
small amber lights glow in a scattered grid, as if lanterns were lit in submerged
courtyards. The lights are diffused by depth and water. Everything else is near black
except faint sky reflection. Amber is the only warm color in frame. Photorealistic,
no people, no text.
```
