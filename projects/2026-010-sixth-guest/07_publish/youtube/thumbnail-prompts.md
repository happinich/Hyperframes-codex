# 썸네일 생성 프롬프트

## A안: CCTV의 여섯 번째 인물

- 문구: `5명 체크인` / `CCTV엔 6명`
- 구도: 왼쪽 문구, 오른쪽 낡은 CCTV 모니터
- 핵심 오브젝트: 입구에 모인 다섯 실루엣과 뒤쪽에 분리된 검은 코트 실루엣
- 색상: 미드나이트 블루, 얼음빛 청록, 경고용 붉은색

```text
Create a cinematic Korean horror YouTube thumbnail background. A snowy night
outside an isolated traditional Korean hanok pension. An old CCTV monitor fills
the right half. On the monitor, show exactly five faceless silhouettes grouped
near the entrance and one clearly separate black-coated sixth silhouette behind
them. Keep the left side dark and simple for headline text. Photorealistic,
high-contrast, ominous blue-black lighting, warm porch light, restrained red REC
accent. No text, logo, watermark, gore, visible faces, or extra people.
```

## B안: 여섯 개의 물컵

- 문구: `다섯 명인데` / `컵은 6개`
- 구도: 왼쪽 식탁과 여섯 잔, 오른쪽 문구
- 핵심 오브젝트: 빠져나온 빈 의자, 붉은 실이 묶인 물컵, 창밖 검은 코트
- 색상: 차콜 블랙, 콜드 틸, 탁한 앰버, 붉은색

```text
Create a photorealistic Korean supernatural-thriller YouTube thumbnail
background. Inside an old hanok pension dining room at night, place exactly six
clear water glasses on a long wooden table and emphasize one empty chair pulled
away by itself. Tie a thin red thread around the closest glass. Through a wet
window, show one distant black-coated silhouette with no visible face. Keep the
right side dark and uncluttered for headline text. No text, logo, watermark,
gore, extra figures, or extra glasses.
```
